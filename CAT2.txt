                    CONTINUOUS ASSESSMENT TEST - II (CAT-II)
================================================================================
Important Guidelines for CAT-II (Open Text Book / Open Note Book Mode):

1. Mode of Examination: Conducted in Open Text Book/Open Note Book mode.
2. Examination Coverage: Covers Modules 3 and 4 across all registered courses.
3. Class Attendance and Notes: Maintaining comprehensive class notes is essential for preparation.
4. Permitted Materials: Students may bring a maximum of two prescribed textbooks, printouts of e-books (complete books or relevant chapters), and their own handwritten notes into the examination hall.
5. Sharing of Materials: Sharing or exchanging any study materials among students inside the examination hall is strictly prohibited.
6. Prohibited Materials: Photocopies of handwritten notes/content and presentation materials (PPT slides) are not permitted inside the examination hall.
7. Attendance Requirement: A minimum of 75% attendance in each registered course is mandatory.
8. Higher-Order Thinking Skills (HOTS): CAT-II questions assess Knowledge Levels KL04 to KL06 (Analyze, Evaluate, Create). Concepts require application rather than rote memorization.
9. Weightage: Maximum 50 Marks (scaled down to 15 Marks, representing 15% of the total course composite).

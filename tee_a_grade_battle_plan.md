## How Relative Grading Works (Quick Refresher)

**A Grade** = Your composite total ≥ **Mean + 0.5σ** (roughly top ~30% of the class)  
**B Grade** = Your composite total ≥ **Mean - 0.5σ** (roughly next ~35–40% of the class)

Using typical VIT Bhopal end-of-semester stats:
- **Course Mean (μ)** ≈ 70–73 / 100
- **Standard Deviation (σ)** ≈ 11–13
- **A-Grade cutoff** ≈ μ + 0.5σ ≈ **76–78 / 100**
- **B-Grade cutoff** ≈ μ - 0.5σ ≈ **63–65 / 100**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> TEE alone is **30% of your total grade** — 70% is already locked pre-TEE. Every 10 marks in raw TEE moves your final course total by **3.0 marks**.

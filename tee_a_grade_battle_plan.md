## How A Grade Works (Quick Refresher)

**A Grade** = Your total ≥ **Mean + 0.5σ** (roughly top ~30% of the class)

Using typical VIT Bhopal end-of-semester stats:
- **Course Mean (μ)** ≈ 73–75 / 100
- **Standard Deviation (σ)** ≈ 7.5–8.5
- **A-Grade cutoff** ≈ μ + 0.5σ ≈ **77–79 / 100**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> TEE alone is **30% of your total grade** — more than CAT1 + CAT2 combined. This is your biggest lever.

- Only **-1.5 behind** class — very recoverable!


### 7. Discrete Math (Oct 21) — TEE Target: **72–75** (for B grade)
- **Guaranteed 60 marks** from 6 purely mechanical algorithms:

| Algorithm | Marks | What You Do |
|-----------|-------|-------------|
| Kruskal's / Prim's MST | 10M | Sort edges, pick smallest non-cycle |
| Havel-Hakimi degree sequence | 5–10M | Repeated subtraction |
| Hasse diagram of D_n | 10M | Find divisors, draw, compute GCD/LCM |
| Dijkstra's shortest path | 10M | Fill a table row by row |
| Congruence modulo proof | 5–10M | 3 memorized steps |
| Logical equivalence laws | 5M | Apply De Morgan, Distribution, etc. |

> [!TIP]
> Don't try to study everything. Nail these 6 templates = guaranteed 60/100 = safe B grade = CGPA protected.

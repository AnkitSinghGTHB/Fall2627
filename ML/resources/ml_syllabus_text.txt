Course Objectives: 
 To introduces theoretical foundations, algorithms, methodologies 
 To introduce the various applications of Machine Learning 
 To provide basic knowledge for handling and analyzing data sets covering a variety of real-world 
applications. 
Course Outcomes: 
At the end of the course, students should able to 
 Recognize the characteristics of machine learning that make it useful to solve real-world 
problems. 
 Identify real-world applications of machine learning. 
 Identify and apply appropriate machine  learning  algorithms for analyzing the data for 
variety of problems. 
 Implement different machine learning algorithms for analyzing the data 
 Design test procedures in order to evaluate a model 
 Combine several models in order to gain better results 
 Make choices for a model for new machine learning tasks based on reasoned argument 
Student Outcomes (SO): a,b,c,l 
a. An ability to apply the knowledge of mathematics, science and computing appropriate  to the discipline 
b. An ability to analyze a problem, identify and define the computing requirements appropriate to its 
solution. 
c. An ability to design, implement and evaluate a system / computer‐based system, process, component or  
program to meet desired needs 
l. An ability to apply mathematical foundations, algorithmic principles and computer science theory in the 
modelling and design of computer-based systems (CS)

### Questions

#### Question 1 (10 Marks)
A hospital wants to predict whether a patient is at high or low risk of a disease using clinical attributes. Design and explain the implementation of Random Forest and AdaBoost for this problem. Compare their training process, prediction mechanism, computational requirements, and expected performance. Recommend one algorithm with justification.

---

#### Question 2 (10 Marks)
Consider a one-dimensional data set $\{5, 9, 14, 25, 28, 40\}$. Perform hierarchical clustering to determine the clusters using single-linkage and complete-linkage analysis, and plot the respective dendrograms to visualize the results. Suggest some key inferences to distinguish between those two analyses.

---

#### Question 3 (10 Marks)
Using K-means clustering, cluster the following data set $D$ into two clusters:

$$D = \{3, 5, 11, 12, 19, 21, 29, 32, 45, 48\}$$

Explore your step-by-step computational analysis using both computational methods, along with key descriptions.

The loan approval dataset is given for 12 applicants:

| Applicant | Income | Credit History | Employment | Existing Loan | Class |

| A3 | Medium | Good | Stable | No | Approved |
| A4 | High | Poor | Stable | Yes | Rejected |
| A5 | Low | Good | Stable | Yes | Rejected |
| A6 | Medium | Poor | Unstable | Yes | Rejected |
| A7 | Low | Poor | Unstable | Yes | Rejected |
| A8 | Medium | Good | Stable | No | Approved |
| A9 | High | Good | Stable | Yes | Approved |
| A10 | Low | Good | Unstable | No | Rejected |
| A11 | Medium | Poor | Stable | Yes | Rejected |
| A12 | High | Poor | Unstable | No | Rejected |

Consider a new applicant $X$ as given below:

$$X = (\text{Income} = \text{Medium}, \, \text{Credit} = \text{Good}, \, \text{Employment} = \text{Stable}, \, \text{Loan} = \text{No})$$

Use the Naïve Bayes Classifier to predict whether the applicant's loan should be Approved or Rejected. Do you think attributes such as income, employment stability, and existing loans are truly independent?

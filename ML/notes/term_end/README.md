## 📋 TEE Exam Paper Architecture (Official Format)
* **Duration**: 3 Hours (Closed Book) | **Total Marks**: 100 Marks (Scaled to 30% Weightage)
* **Section A (60 Marks)**: 5 Questions × 12 Marks (1 from each Unit; internal choice `OR` format, either 12M or 6+6M)
* **Section B (40 Marks)**: 5 Questions × 8 Marks (1 from each Unit; direct numerical/algorithm question without subparts)
* **Weight Distribution**: Every Unit carries **exactly 20 Marks** (12M in Sec A + 8M in Sec B).

## 🏆 Scoring Rubric for Numerical & Algorithmic ML Questions
1. **Always write the formal mathematical equation first** (e.g., Entropy $H(S) = -\sum p_i \log_2 p_i$, Bayes Rule, Euclidean Distance).
2. **Tabulate step-by-step iterations** (e.g., K-Means centroid updates, Apriori candidate itemset frequency counts).
3. **Show final classification or cluster assignment explicitly**.

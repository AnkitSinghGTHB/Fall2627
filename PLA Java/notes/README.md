## 💡 Study Strategy for PLA

### The reality:
- **70% of the marks** = Writing correct programs for given problems
- **20% of the marks** = Understanding logic (loops, conditions, recursion)
- **10% of the marks** = Theory (OOP concepts, etc.)

### The approach:
- 🎯 **Understand 10 patterns** (they recycle the same patterns in different questions)
- 🎯 **Don't memorize code** — understand the *template* and fill in the blanks
- 🎯 **Practice on paper** — exams are handwritten, not IDE-typed

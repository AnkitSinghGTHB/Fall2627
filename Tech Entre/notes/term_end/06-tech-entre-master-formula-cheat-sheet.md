## 1. The 9 Blocks of Osterwalder's Business Model Canvas (BMC)
1. **Customer Segments (CS)**: Who we create value for (Mass, Niche, Multi-sided).
2. **Value Propositions (VP)**: What bundle of products solves their specific pain.
3. **Channels (CH)**: Touchpoints used to communicate and deliver VP (Direct, Retail, App).
4. **Customer Relationships (CR)**: Type of relationship (Self-service, Dedicated, Co-creation).
5. **Revenue Streams (RS)**: How cash is captured (Asset sale, Usage, Subscription, Brokerage).
6. **Key Resources (KR)**: Essential assets (Physical, Intellectual, Human, Financial).
7. **Key Activities (KA)**: Critical operations (Production, Problem solving, Platform).
8. **Key Partnerships (KP)**: Strategic allies (Buyer-supplier, Non-compete alliances, JVs).
9. **Cost Structure (CS)**: All operational costs (Fixed, Variable, Economies of scale).

## 2. Customer Discovery & Validation Frameworks
* **Steve Blank's 4 Steps**:
  $$\text{State Hypotheses} \longrightarrow \text{Test Problem} \longrightarrow \text{Test Solution} \longrightarrow \text{Verify \& Pivot / Proceed}$$
* **The 4 Pillars of Feasibility**:
  1. *Product / Service Feasibility* (Concept test + MVP demand test)
  2. *Industry / Target Market Feasibility* (Porter's 5 Forces + TAM $\rightarrow$ SAM $\rightarrow$ SOM)
  3. *Organizational Feasibility* (Founder-market fit + Resource sufficiency)
  4. *Financial Feasibility* (Startup cash required + Unit economics: $LTV \ge 3 \times CAC$)

## 3. Intellectual Property Rights (IPR) Comparison Cheat Sheet

| IPR Form | Protects | India Term | Core Condition |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Patent** | Novel functional inventions | **20 Years** | Novelty + Inventive step + Industrial utility |
| **Trademark** | Brand name, logo, trade dress | **10 Yrs (Renewable)** | Distinctive, non-deceptive, non-generic |
| **Copyright** | Source code, books, music | **Author Life + 60 Yrs** | Originality of expression in tangible form |
| **Trade Secret** | Confidential formulas, code | **Indefinite** | Commercial value from secrecy + Non-disclosure |
| **Industrial Design** | External ornamental appearance | **10 + 5 Yrs (Max 15)** | Novelty in visual eye-appeal (non-functional) |

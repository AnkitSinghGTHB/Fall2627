## ✍️ Exam Hall Writing Strategy & Faculty Grading Psychology

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Faculty Grading Psychology (From your CAT-1 & CAT-2 experience)**:
> Dr. Abhishek Raj heavily rewards answers that are **visually well-organized, spacious, and fill the booklet pages properly with clear handwriting**. When writing fast, rushed messy cursive loses marks even if the factual points are correct.

### The 4 Rules to Maximize Presentation Marks:
1. **Never Cram Words**: Write with generous line spacing (leave 1 blank line between subheadings). Use bullet points with indented text.
2. **Double-Underline Headings & Box Diagrams**: Draw boxes around frameworks (like the 9-Block BMC, 4 Feasibility Pillars, and S-Curves). Evaluators flip pages looking for visual landmarks.
3. **Target Page Count**:
   - **Section A (12-Mark Question)**: **2.5 to 3 full pages**. (Start with Boxed Definition ➔ Large Framework Grid / Comparison Table ➔ 5–6 Detailed Bolded Points ➔ Real-world Case Study).
   - **Section B (8-Mark Question)**: **1.5 to 2 full pages**. (Definition ➔ 4–5 Bolded Points / Comparison Matrix ➔ Real Startup Example).
4. **Pacing without Ruining Handwriting**:
   - Section A (5 questions): **22 mins per question** (110 mins total).
   - Section B (5 questions): **12 mins per question** (60 mins total).
   - If your hand starts tiring, switch to bulleted lists and tables—tables take up more page space and force slower, legible lettering!

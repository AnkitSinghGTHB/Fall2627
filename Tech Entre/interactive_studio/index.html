CAT-2 / FAT MASTER CASE

EcoTech Solutions vs AquaSmart Ltd

EcoTech created an IoT smart purifier with automated filter notifications. They filed an Indian patent application and registered their brand logo.

6 months later, AquaSmart launched an identical purifier with the same IoT mechanism and a confusingly similar brand logo. Advise EcoTech on legal remedies.

1. Can EcoTech sue for Patent Infringement?➔
2. Trademark Action: Deceptive Similarity➔
3. Firmware Code: Which IPR applies?➔
4. Future IPR Protection Roadmap➔

⚖️ Patent Law: Section 11A

Answer: NO (Not yet). EcoTech only has a patent application (Pending status). Under Section 11A of the Patents Act 1970, an infringement suit cannot be filed until the patent is granted.

Remedy: File Form 18A for Expedited Examination. Once granted, EcoTech can claim retrospective damages backdated to the publication date!

Steve Blank's Customer Discovery

The 4 steps: State hypotheses, Test problem, Test solution, Pivot or proceed.

1. State Hypotheses: Document assumptions across all 9 BMC blocks.

2. Test Problem: Interview 50–100 potential customers. Use The Mom Test: ask about past actions, never hypothetical promises.

3. Test Solution: Show prototype/MVP to measure real engagement.

4. Pivot or Proceed: If data disproves value proposition, execute a structured pivot.

8 MARKS

TAM / SAM / SOM Market Sizing

Definitions and concentric hierarchy calculation.

• TAM (Total Addressable Market): 100% global demand (e.g. $100B global market).

• SAM (Serviceable Available Market): Segment reachable within geography (e.g. $10B India).

• SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market): Realistic 2–3 year capture (e.g. 5% of SAM = $500M).

Valuation & Runway Math

Pre-money, Post-money, and Burn Rate formulas.

$$\text{Post-Money} = \text{Pre-Money} + \text{Investment Raised}$$

$$\text{Investor Equity \%} = \frac{\text{Investment}}{\text{Post-Money}} \times 100$$

$$\text{Runway (Months)} = \frac{\text{Cash in Bank}}{\text{Monthly Net Burn}}$$

                    CONTINUOUS ASSESSMENT TEST - I (CAT-I)
================================================================================
1. Exam Scope & Structure:
   - Coverage: Modules 1 and 2 for every registered course.
   - Question Types: Primarily Knowledge Level 1 (KL01 - Remember) and 
     Knowledge Level 2 (KL02 - Understand).
   - Mode: Closed book.
   - Maximum Marks: 50 Marks (scaled down to 15 Marks).
   - Weightage: 15% of the total course composite.

2. Registered Courses in CAT-I:
   - MAT2002: Discrete Mathematics and Graph Theory
   - ECE2002: Digital Logic Design
   - CSE4005: Machine Learning
   - PLA1004: Competitive Coding Practices
   - CSE3011: Python Programming
   - MGT2003: Technology Entrepreneurship
   - CSE2004: Theory Of Computation And Compiler Design

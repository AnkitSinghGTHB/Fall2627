1. TEE PAPER STRUCTURE:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Duration     : 3 Hours (Closed Book)
- Total Marks  : 100 Marks (Scaled to 30% Weightage -> Max 30 Marks)
- Section A    : 5 Questions x 12 Marks = 60 Marks
                 (1 Question per module, internal choice "OR" format, e.g. 12 or 12, or split 6 + 6)
- Section B    : 5 Questions x 8 Marks = 40 Marks
                 (1 Question per module, direct question without subparts)
- Distribution : Every module (1 to 5) has exactly 20 marks (12M in Section A + 8M in Section B).
- Difficulty   : Knowledge Levels KL01/02 up to KL03/04 (comprehensive problem solving).
- Passing Rule : Mandatory minimum of 40/100 in TEE (>= 12 / 30 weighted).

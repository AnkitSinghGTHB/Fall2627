1. TEE / FAT EXAM PAPER STRUCTURE (OFFICIAL FORMAT FROM TEE.txt)
================================================================================
Total Marks : 100 Marks (3-Hour Duration | Closed Book)
Course Weightage : 30% of total grade (Scaled: TEE_Score * 0.30 -> Max 30 Marks)

Paper Layout:
  - Section A : 5 Questions x 12 Marks = 60 Marks
                (1 Question per module, internal choice "OR" format, e.g. 12 or 12,
                or split as 6 + 6)
  - Section B : 5 Questions x 8 Marks = 40 Marks
                (1 Question per module, direct question without subparts)
  - Distribution: Every single module (1 to 5) has exactly 20 marks (12M + 8M).
  - Difficulty  : Between CAT-1 (basic KL01/KL02) and CAT-2 (HOTS KL03/KL04).

2. OFFICIAL VIT BHOPAL RELATIVE GRADING FORMULA & CUTOFFS (grading.txt)
================================================================================
Total Course Composite (100) =
    (CAT-1 * 0.30) [15M] + (CAT-2 * 0.30) [15M] + Internals [35M] + Attendance [5M] + (TEE * 0.30) [30M]

Relative Grading Cutoffs (Class Mean = mu, Standard Deviation = sigma):
  - S Grade : Total >= (mu + 1.5*sigma) with a minimum of 90% total
  - A Grade : Total >= (mu + 0.5*sigma) and < (mu + 1.5*sigma)  [Top ~30% of class]
  - B Grade : Total >= (mu - 0.5*sigma) and < (mu + 0.5*sigma)  [Middle ~38% of class]
  - C Grade : Total >= (mu - 1.0*sigma) and < (mu - 0.5*sigma)
  - D Grade : Total >= (mu - 1.5*sigma) and < (mu - 1.0*sigma)
  - E Grade : Total >= (mu - 2.0*sigma) and < (mu - 1.5*sigma)
  - F Grade : Total <  (mu - 2.0*sigma) OR Total < 50 OR TEE < 40/100

Mandatory Passing Rules:
  1. TEE Component Minimum: Must score at least 40 out of 100 in TEE (>= 12 / 30).
  2. Course Aggregate Minimum: Final composite total aggregate must be >= 50 / 100.

4. KEY EXAM REVELATIONS & CRITICAL INSIGHTS
================================================================================
1. RELATIVE GRADING S-GRADE RULE:
   - S Grade requires meeting BOTH conditions: Total >= (Mean + 1.5*sigma) AND Total >= 90%.

2. THE TOC MANDATORY 6-PART FORMAT (DR. GAURAV SONI):
   - High step marks require following this structured rubric:
       Part 1: Language Description (Formal set builder notation)
       Part 2: Design Logic (Algorithm in plain English)
       Part 3: Formal Tuples (Explicit enumeration of Q, Sigma, Gamma, etc.)
       Part 4: State Transition Table (delta matrix)
       Part 5: State Transition Diagram (Clean graph with start & final states)
       Part 6: Validation (Instantaneous Description |- trace for a test string)

3. DISCRETE MATH 6 CORE MECHANICAL TEMPLATES:
   - 60+ marks come from repeatable algorithmic procedures:
       1) Kruskal's / Prim's MST table (edge sorting & cycle checking)
       2) Havel-Hakimi graphic degree sequence reduction (subtraction sorting)
       3) Hasse diagram of Dn with GLB(gcd) and LUB(lcm)
       4) Dijkstra shortest path table (node status & distance updates)
       5) Equivalence relation proofs (Reflexive, Symmetric, Transitive)
       6) Propositional logic laws & normal forms (PDNF / PCNF)
